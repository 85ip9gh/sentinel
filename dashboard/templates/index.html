This page reads from the HDFS archive rather than from the Kafka stream, so readings appear one sink batch behind live and every row shows its own age.

Python, Kafka in KRaft mode, single-node HDFS, and Cloudflare Tunnel. Collectors buffer readings to local disk when the broker is unreachable and replay them in order once it returns.
